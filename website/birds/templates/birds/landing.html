About Birds of Paradise

Birds of Paradise is an AI-powered biodiversity platform dedicated to the birds of Jammu, Kashmir and Ladakh.

The platform combines ecological databases, migratory bird tracking, species galleries, and artificial intelligence to document and preserve Himalayan avian diversity.

Why Migratory Birds Visit Kashmir

Kashmir lies along the Central Asian Flyway, one of the world's major migratory bird routes.

Every winter thousands of birds migrate from Siberia, Central Asia, Europe and the Tibetan Plateau to the wetlands and lakes of Kashmir.

Wetlands such as Hokersar, Wular Lake, Hygam and Shallabugh provide food, shelter and breeding habitats for ducks, geese, cranes, gulls and many other migratory species.
