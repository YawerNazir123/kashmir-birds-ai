About Birds of Paradise

Birds of Paradise is a project about the birds of Jammu, Kashmir and Ladakh, built to document them and keep that record alive.

It brings together a species database, migration records, photo galleries and an AI identification tool, all in one place, so the region's birdlife is documented and easy to explore.

Kashmiri local names on this site are sourced from “Birds of Jammu and Kashmir” by Parvaiz Yousuf.

Why Migratory Birds Visit Kashmir

Kashmir sits on the Central Asian Flyway, one of the great migratory routes of the world.

Every winter, thousands of birds make the long journey here from Siberia, Central Asia, Europe and the Tibetan Plateau, settling on Kashmir's wetlands and lakes.

Hokersar, Wular Lake, Hygam, Shallabugh and other wetlands give them food and shelter through the cold months. Ducks, geese, cranes, gulls and many more spend the winter here.
