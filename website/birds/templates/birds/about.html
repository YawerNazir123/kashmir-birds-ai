About Birds of Paradise

AI-Powered Biodiversity Platform for Jammu, Kashmir & Ladakh

Our Mission

Birds of Paradise is a biodiversity and conservation platform focused on documenting the avian diversity of Jammu, Kashmir and Ladakh.

The project combines artificial intelligence, ecological data, species databases and wildlife photography to create a modern digital archive of Himalayan birds.

The Central Asian Flyway

Kashmir lies along the Central Asian Flyway, one of the world's most important migratory bird routes.

Every year thousands of migratory birds travel from Siberia, Central Asia, Europe and high-altitude Himalayan regions to the wetlands of Kashmir.

Wetlands such as Hokersar, Wular Lake, Hygam and Shallabugh serve as critical habitats for ducks, cranes, geese, gulls and several endangered bird species.
