The people and the book behind Birds of Paradise

Who Made This Possible

Birds of Paradise is the work of one developer, but it stands on the knowledge, photographs and support of many people — above all the author of the book this site is built on, and the conservationists and researchers of Kashmir who documented its birds long before this website existed.

This page thanks them: the three people at the heart of the project, everyone the book thanks by name, and the hundreds of photographers whose pictures made the book possible.

People Behind the Project

Parvaiz Yousuf

Author, Birds of Jammu & Kashmir

Author of “Birds of Jammu & Kashmir” (2023), the field guide this site is built on. The book gave the site its Kashmiri, Dogri and Ladakhi local names, its sizes, IUCN status and descriptions.

Parvaiz is a PhD student at James Cook University, Australia, in Ecology & Evolutionary Biology.

YouTube

Nadeem Qadri

Environmental Lawyer & Conservationist

Environmental lawyer and conservationist from Pampore, Kashmir. Founder of The Nature University (Pampore) and the Wildlife Conservation Fund; Amicus Curiae to the High Court of Jammu & Kashmir; Member of the Green India Mission (2023); “Guardian of the Himalayas” award, July 2026.

He is thanked in the book’s vote of thanks.

Facebook

Aadil Bhat

Bird Researcher, Kashmir

Bird researcher from Kashmir. His work includes published studies on the avifauna of Rajparian Wildlife Sanctuary, Kashmir, cited in the book’s bibliography.

Facebook
